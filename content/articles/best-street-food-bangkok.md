Bangkok might be the world's greatest street food city, and the critics agree on a handful of places that are worth the queue.

## 1. Raan Jay Fai: crab omelette and drunken noodles

Chef Jay Fai cooks over charcoal in her trademark ski goggles. Her shop at 327 Maha Chai Road holds one Michelin star, retained in the 2026 Thailand guide, and featured in Netflix's *Street Food: Asia*. Michelin singles out the crispy crab omelette and the yellow-curry prawns. It's open Wednesday to Saturday only. Expect a long queue and bring cash.

## 2. Thipsamai: pad thai

A Maha Chai Road institution, trading as Thipsamai since 1966. It's best known for its signature pad thai with prawn oil, wrapped in a thin egg skin. It's often called "Michelin", but it isn't on the current 2026 Bib Gourmand list.

## 3. Yaowarat (Chinatown) after dark

When the sun sets, Yaowarat Road fills with stalls. Guides consistently recommend it for grazing: seafood, noodles, toasted buns and sweets, all within a few blocks.

## 4. Nai Mong Hoi Tod: oyster omelette

A small shop on Soi Phlap Phla Chai in Chinatown, on Michelin's 2026 Bib Gourmand list. It's known for crispy oyster omelette (hoi tod) and mussel omelette.

## Good to know

Bring cash, go early or late to dodge peak queues, and check opening days, since many stalls close one day a week.
