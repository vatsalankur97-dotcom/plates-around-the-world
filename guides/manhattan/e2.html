    Episode 2 · Wed 9 July 2025

    Manhattan

    One Broadway night, for about $144.

    Hell's Kitchen, The Lion King and Times Square after dark. After the bus day, one more night: one bar, one show, and one square that's bright by law. From 5:55 PM to 10:15 PM.

    About $144 for the night, per person. About $17 for one cocktail and about $127 for a Lion King ticket. About ₹12,400. Estimates, not receipts.

    Our own photos and video, 9 July 2025. AI-narrated video.

    Food · 5:55 PM

    A bar on 9th Avenue, Hell's Kitchen

    A cocktail on 9th Avenue in Hell's Kitchen. The name first hit print in 1881. In 1959 the city tried to swap it for Clinton.

    About $17 for one cocktail. Estimate, not a receipt. Running total about $17.

    Sources: Hell's Kitchen, Wikipedia.

    6:42 PM

    AMC Empire 25

    02

    Place · 6:42 PM

    AMC Empire 25, 42nd Street

    The AMC Empire 25 on 42nd Street. Its lobby is the 1912 Eltinge Theatre, rolled 168 feet down 42nd Street in 1998.

    Sources: Eltinge 42nd Street Theatre, Wikipedia.

    Carved wooden lion mask on a red wall at the Minskoff Theatre

    03

    Place · 6:48 PM

    The Minskoff Theatre

    The Minskoff opened in 1973. Part of the tower rests on its roof. The Lion King has played there since 2006.

    Sources: Minskoff Theatre, Wikipedia · The Lion King (musical), Wikipedia.

    Carved wooden lion mask on a red wall at the Minskoff Theatre

    04

    Show · 7:00 PM

    The Lion King, curtain up

    Curtain up on The Lion King. Julie Taymor was the first woman to win the Tony for directing a musical. We don't show the stage.

    About $127 for the ticket. Today's starting price, so check before you book. Estimate, not a receipt. Running total about $144.

    Sources: The Lion King (musical), Wikipedia. The about $127 figure is today's starting price on Broadway ticket listings.

    Why Times Square glows

    The signs have been required by zoning since 1987. This is the square that's bright by law.

    Sources: Times Square, Wikipedia · NYC Zoning Resolution, Special Times Square signage requirements.
