About us

Plates Around the World started with a simple question every traveller asks: where should we eat? We're here to answer it like a well-travelled friend would: warmly, curiously, and honestly.

We publish city restaurant guides, street food round-ups and home recipes so you can taste the world on the road or in your own kitchen.

How we research. Unless a guide is clearly marked "First-hand visit", it is researched from public ratings and press coverage, such as the Michelin Guide, respected food media and well-known review platforms. We name our sources at the end of every guide. We never present research as a personal visit. Opening hours and prices change, so please check before you go.

Spotted something out of date? Tell us and we'll fix it.
