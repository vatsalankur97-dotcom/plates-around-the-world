    Butter chicken comes from Delhi's Punjabi restaurant kitchens, and who invented it is literally a court case. Moti Mahal credits its founder Kundan Lal Gujral, while the Daryaganj chain says his partner Kundan Lal Jaggi created it. The Delhi High Court is hearing the dispute. Our version keeps the char and the silky sauce.

    Ingredients

    Marinade

    • 700 g boneless chicken thighs, in chunks
    • 200 g thick yoghurt
    • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
    • 1½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
    • 1 tsp garam masala
    • 1 tsp salt, juice of ½ lemon

    Sauce

    • 3 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil
    • 1 onion, finely chopped (optional)
    • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
    • 400 g tomato purée
    • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder, 1 tsp garam masala
    • 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
    • 1 tsp sugar or honey
    • 120 ml double cream
    • Salt to taste; coriander to garnish

    Notes and swaps: OWNER TO SUPPLY

    Method

    1. Marinate. Mix the marinade ingredients with the chicken and rest it for at least 1 hour, or overnight in the fridge.
    2. Char. Grill, air-fry or sear the chicken in a very hot pan until charred at the edges and just cooked. Set aside.
    3. Build the sauce. Melt the butter with the oil, soften the onion for 6–8 min, then add the ginger-garlic paste for 1 min.
    4. Simmer. Add the tomato purée, chilli and garam masala. Cook for 10–12 min until it thickens and the butter separates at the edges. Blend smooth if you like.
    5. Finish. Stir in the chicken, the crushed kasuri methi, sugar and cream. Simmer for 5 min and season.
    6. Serve with naan or jeera rice, a knob of butter and some coriander.
