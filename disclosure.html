    Disclosure: how we make money

    Plates Around the World is a small, independent food publication. We keep it free to read in a few honest ways, and we'll always tell you which one is in play.

    Affiliate links. Some pages link to products on shops such as Amazon.in, Flipkart and other retailers, through affiliate programmes and networks (for example EarnKaro and Cuelinks). If you buy through one of these links, we may earn a small commission. It costs you nothing extra. Pages with affiliate links carry an "Affiliate" note before the first link. We only list gear and ingredients we actually use, or clearly say when we haven't tried something.

    Gifts, hosted meals and collaborations. Sometimes a brand sends us a product, or a restaurant hosts our meal. When that happens, the post is labelled "Free gift" or "Collaboration" at the top and says exactly what we received. Our opinions stay our own, and nobody gets to approve what we write.

    Paid partnerships. Paid content is labelled "Ad" or "Sponsored" at the top, and uses the platform's paid-partnership tag on social media.

    First-hand vs researched. Separately from all this, every guide says whether we ate there ourselves ("First-hand visit") or researched it ("Researched guide"). See How we research.

    AI. We don't use AI-generated images to show a partner's product or a restaurant's food. If an image in a post is AI-generated, we label it.

    We follow the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) guidelines for influencer advertising. Questions? Email plates.arounddtheworld@gmail.com.
